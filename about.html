About Us

We are your friendly neighborhood bakery, located on 123 Rainbow Road. Since 1982, we've served warm baked goods and friendly smiles!

Sustainable Sourcing

We source all of our ingredients from local farms and businesses. We verify every vendor using a "farm to shelf" methodology that includes regular audits and vendor attestation. If you'd like to know more about any of our ingredients, simply scan the QR code on your product label to see where each ingredient came from.
